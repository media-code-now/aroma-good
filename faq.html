A few good answers.

From choosing your first scent to understanding the subscription, start here.

Is the Column diffuser really free?

Yes. The Column diffuser is included at $0 with the $29.99/month scent subscription, reduced from $39.99/month. This is a recurring scent plan; it is not a one-time $29.99 diffuser purchase.

What is included?

The Column, one 200 ml starter scent, power supply, weighted base, and quick-start card. Choose Steel, Charcoal, or Cream and one of five fragrances.

Can I start a paid subscription now?

Not yet. Checkout currently previews your selection without taking payment, placing an order, or enrolling you. We will publish the refill schedule, commitment period, and cancellation conditions before enrollment opens.

Does monthly billing mean monthly refills?

The plan is billed monthly. The refill delivery schedule has not yet been finalized. A cartridge’s estimated lifespan is separate from the billing and shipping schedules.

Can I cancel and keep the free diffuser?

Minimum commitment, cancellation, and diffuser-retention conditions are still being finalized. We are not promising a cancel-anytime plan or that the diffuser can be kept after cancellation. These conditions will be available before enrollment.

Will the Column fit my space?

The freestanding Column covers up to 1,500 sq ft. Layout and airflow affect coverage. Use the sizing tool or ask for help.

How do I choose a fragrance?

Choose First Light for green freshness, Liquid Sun for citrus, Santal for warm wood, The Unwinding for lavender and soft smoke, or White Tea for a clean, airy feel. Explore all five.

How does it work?

Cold-air diffusion turns fragrance oil into a dry mist without heating it or adding water. Set output and a weekly schedule through the app.

How long does a cartridge last?

At moderate output, a 200 ml cartridge is estimated to last about 90 days in the Column. Your output setting, schedule, and space affect usage.

Where do you ship?

The published offer includes free standard shipping in the continental US. See shipping information for handling and transit estimates.

What is the return policy?

The stated product return window is 30 days with the scent cartridge unopened. Defective-product return shipping is covered. Subscription cancellation and refunds will have separate terms before enrollment. Read the return details.

How do I contact you?

Email hello@aroma7.com for product questions, delivery help, returns, or business scenting.
