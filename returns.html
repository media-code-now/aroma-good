30-day product returns

The published product return policy allows a return within 30 days of delivery with the scent cartridge unopened. Contact us within that period to begin a return and describe the condition of the diffuser and cartridge.

Opening the cartridge affects eligibility for a change-of-mind return. This condition does not remove rights that apply to a defective product.

Start a return

  1. Email hello@aroma7.com with your order reference, purchase email, and reason for return.
  2. Wait for return instructions and the confirmed return address.
  3. Pack the diffuser, accessories, and unopened cartridge securely. Keep your shipment receipt and tracking information.

Do not ship to an address that has not been confirmed by support.

Shipping costs & refunds

We cover return shipping for defective products. For other returns, support will confirm any return-shipping cost before you send the product.

Eligible product returns receive a refund of the amount paid for the returned product. We will explain the refund amount and any treatment of original delivery charges when arranging the return. Bank or payment-provider processing time may vary.

Free diffuser & scent subscriptions

The Column is $0 as part of a recurring $29.99/month scent plan. Returning hardware and canceling recurring billing are separate actions. The minimum commitment, cancellation process, refill refund eligibility, and whether the diffuser must be returned after cancellation have not yet been finalized.

Paid enrollment is not open. These conditions will be shown before enrollment; do not assume a cancel-anytime offer or a right to keep the diffuser after cancellation.

2-year warranty

The published diffuser warranty covers the atomiser and electronics for two years. For a suspected fault, email your order reference, a description, and photos or video if helpful. Support will assess the issue and explain the next step.

Warranty service is separate from the 30-day return policy. These policies do not limit consumer rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.
