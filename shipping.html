If we cannot ship a paid order within the promised time, we will contact you with a revised estimate and the option to accept the delay or cancel the unshipped order for a refund.

For a missing or damaged delivery, email hello@aroma7.com with your order reference and details. Include photos when available. Contact us promptly if the shipping address needs correction; changes may not be possible after dispatch.
