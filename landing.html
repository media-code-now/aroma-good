What does "reserve" actually mean?

You're not charged today. Reserving locks the founding price ($349 instead of $449) for 30 days while we finish the first production run. You decide later whether to buy.

Is Aroma Good a real, shipping product?

Yes. This is the first production run of a physical device, not a concept — we're limiting it to 500 units while we scale manufacturing. Columns ship within 3 business days of your order.

Is it safe around people and pets?

The oils are IFRA-compliant and diffused at very low concentration. Nothing is heated and no water is added, so there's no humidity, no plate to burn, and no residue.

Can I return it?

Yes — 30 days, full refund, as long as the scent cartridge is unopened. We cover return shipping on anything defective.

What's the running cost?

A 200 ml cartridge runs about 90 days at moderate output. Refills are $59, or $49 on a subscription you can pause any time.
