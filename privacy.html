Privacy notice

How this version of the Aroma Good website handles information.

This notice describes the website as currently implemented. Live orders and paid subscriptions are not available in this version.

Who this notice covers

This notice covers the Aroma Good website and inquiries sent to hello@aroma7.com. It describes the current website preview. It will need updating when live checkout, subscriptions, or additional service providers are introduced.

Information you provide

If you email us, your email service sends your address, message, and any attachments to our support mailbox. We use that information to respond and handle the request.

The site’s information forms ask for an email address and may ask for diffuser, finish, space, and size preferences. Those forms currently have no connected submission service and do not deliver a request. Use the email contact link instead.

The checkout preview lets you enter contact and delivery information in your browser. It does not submit an order to a payment or order service, collect card details, or start recurring billing. Avoid entering sensitive information into a preview.

Technical information & storage

Visiting a website sends technical request information, such as an IP address and browser information, to the server serving the page and assets. The hosting provider’s logging settings are not specified in this project.

Some pages request fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the technical information needed to serve those files. New information pages use system fonts. Email links open your email application and are governed by your email provider’s practices.

The homepage uses session storage to remember whether its exit popup has been displayed. The cart stores product type, finish, scent, and quantity in this browser’s local storage so your cart remains available across pages and visits. It does not store your email, address, or payment information. Clear site storage to remove the saved cart. When browser storage is unavailable, the checkout link carries product selections in its URL instead. Product selections appear in page URLs and may remain in your browser history. The current site does not set advertising cookies or load an active analytics service. If analytics or advertising services are connected later, this notice must be updated before use.

Use & sharing

Information in an inquiry is used to answer questions, assist with a product, or respond to a return or privacy request. Email and hosting providers process information as part of operating those services. Information may also need to be disclosed when required by applicable law.

The current website has no configured feature for selling personal information or sharing it with an advertising network. Future integrations may change how data is handled and will require updated disclosures.

Your choices & requests

You can browse without submitting a form or sending an email. Clear site storage through your browser settings to remove the popup preference. You can ask about access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us by emailing hello@aroma7.com.

We may need to verify a request before disclosing or changing information. Rights and exceptions depend on applicable law. Please send only information necessary to identify the request; do not send payment card details or passwords.

Retention & updates

A production retention schedule, complete provider list, and legal business identity have not yet been published. Ask us about information in an existing inquiry. We will update this notice as the service and its data practices are finalized.
