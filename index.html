Cold-air diffusion

No heat. No water. No film on the glass.

Reed sticks fade. Plug-ins burn oil and flatten the top notes. Aroma Good atomises undiluted oil into particles small enough to stay suspended in air.

Questions

Before you reserve.

When will it arrive?

Columns ship within 3 business days, Panels within 5. Ground shipping in the continental US takes a further 3 to 5 days and is free. Express is $24 at checkout.

Can I return it?

Yes. Send it back within 30 days for a full refund as long as the scent cartridge is unopened. We cover return shipping on anything defective.

Does the Panel really need HVAC?

Yes. It mounts near your air handler and taps the supply duct, which is how it reaches 12,000 sq ft from one point. Most installs take a technician about 30 minutes. If you don't have central air, the Column is your unit.

Is it safe around people and pets?

The oils are IFRA-compliant and diffused at very low concentration. Nothing is heated and no water is added, so there's no humidity, no plate to burn, and no residue.

Can I use my own fragrance oil?

Cartridges are sealed to protect the atomiser, but we'll produce custom blends for commercial reservations of ten units or more. Mention it in your reply to the confirmation email.

What's the running cost?

A 200 ml cartridge runs about 90 days in a Column at moderate output. Refills are $59, or $49 on a subscription you can pause any time.
